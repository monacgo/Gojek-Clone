## 🔎 Overview

[Zipprr Gojek Clone](https://zipprr.com/gojek-clone/) is a multi-service software offering for entrepreneurs and local marketplace operators evaluating a Gojek-like business model.

Its public product overview describes ride and order bookings, driver/vendor management, real-time tracking and digital payments.

### Who is this solution for?

- Entrepreneurs planning an on-demand marketplace.
- Local businesses connecting customers with service providers.
- Operators exploring multiple services under one brand.
- Existing service businesses considering a shared booking platform.

**Start with a focused market:** decide which services you will offer, where you will operate and who will fulfill customer requests.

## 🧩 Core Modules

A multi-service business brings together three connected perspectives. Use this checklist during your product demonstration to confirm the included components.

| Perspective | Journey to Explore | What to Confirm |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | Discover → Book → Track → Pay | Service availability, booking details and cancellation handling |
| Service Provider | Onboard → Receive → Fulfill → Settle | Provider approval, booking allocation and settlement |
| Business Operator | Configure → Monitor → Review | Available controls, reporting and administrator permissions |

**Demo tip:** Follow one booking across all three perspectives, including an exception such as a cancellation or failed payment.
